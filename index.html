arXiv · 2606.15883

Koshur Diacritizer: A Byte-Level Sequence-to-Sequence Model for Kashmiri Diacritic Restoration

The first dedicated neural model for Kashmiri diacritic restoration. A ByT5 byte-level seq2seq model with script-aware normalization and skeleton-preserving inference recovers omitted Perso-Arabic diacritics. Achieves 77.5% expert-rated accuracy; released a 23.7k aligned sentence-pair dataset.

Low-Resource NLP Diacritization Kashmiri

arXiv · 2606.23144

Koshur Pixel: A Large-Scale Synthetic OCR Dataset for Kashmiri

A comprehensive synthetic OCR dataset for Kashmiri, a low-resource language in Perso-Arabic Nastaliq script. Renders the KS-PRET-5M corpus into 613,078 multi-font, multi-granularity image-text pairs with 25+ real-world degradation augmentations, breaking the annotated-data barrier for Kashmiri OCR.
